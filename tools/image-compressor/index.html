    Frequently asked questions

    Are my images uploaded to a server?

    No. Compression uses your browser’s canvas engine entirely on your device. The file never leaves your computer.

    Why did a PNG get bigger?

    PNG is lossless. Compressing to JPEG or WebP (lossy formats) usually shrinks photos dramatically; converting PNG to PNG mostly helps screenshots with many colors.
