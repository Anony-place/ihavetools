Text Diff

Paste two versions of a text to see added, removed and unchanged lines side by side, computed with a proper longest-common-subsequence diff.

This tool runs entirely in your browser. Your data is never uploaded or stored.

How to use Text Diff

  1. Paste the original text on the left and the changed text on the right.
  2. Click Compare.
  3. Review added (green) and removed (red) lines in the result.

Why use the iHaveTools Text Diff?

  • Private by design — This tool runs entirely in your browser. Your data is never uploaded or stored.
  • Free and unlimited — no sign-up, no watermarks, no daily limits and nothing to install.
  • Works everywhere — any modern browser on a phone, tablet or computer, ready the moment the page opens.
