PDF Split

Extract selected pages or ranges (e.g. 1-3, 7, 11-13) from a PDF into a new document, or split every page into its own file — all in your browser.

Your file is processed locally in your browser and is not uploaded.

How to use PDF Split

  1. Drop or choose a PDF file.
  2. Enter the pages or ranges to extract, or choose “split every page”.
  3. Click Split PDF, then download the result.

Frequently asked questions

Is PDF Split free to use?

Yes — it is completely free, with no sign-up, no watermarks and no usage limits.

Does my data leave my device?

No — your file is processed locally in your browser and is not uploaded.
