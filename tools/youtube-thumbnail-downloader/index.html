    YouTube Thumbnail Downloader

    Paste a YouTube video link (watch, youtu.be, Shorts or embed) or a video ID and download its thumbnail in every available size — max resolution 1280×720, SD, HQ, MQ and default. Images load directly from YouTube’s public CDN.

    Thumbnail images load from YouTube’s image CDN, which receives your IP address and the requested video ID.

    How to use YouTube Thumbnail Downloader

    1. Paste a YouTube video URL or its 11-character video ID.
    2. Click Get thumbnails.
    3. Preview the sizes and download the JPG you need (or copy its URL).

    YouTube Thumbnail Downloader guide

    The sizes YouTube generates for every video

    For every public video, YouTube’s image CDN (i.ytimg.com) pre-renders the thumbnail in fixed sizes — this tool simply builds those URLs from the video ID and loads them straight to your browser:

    Size nameDimensionsNotes
    Maximum (maxresdefault)1280 × 720The full custom thumbnail — only exists if the video/thumbnail was uploaded in HD
    SD (sddefault)640 × 4804:3 frame, may include black bars on wide videos
    HQ (hqdefault)480 × 360Present for virtually every video
    MQ (mqdefault)320 × 18016:9 small preview
    Default120 × 90Tiny fallback that always exists

    Why a size sometimes goes missing

    When a size was never generated — classically, Maximum on old or low-resolution uploads — YouTube does not return an error; it quietly serves a 120 × 90 placeholder image instead. This tool checks each image’s real pixel width as it loads and removes those placeholders, so every card you see is a genuine copy at the stated size. If Maximum is absent, the creator never uploaded an HD thumbnail and the best available is usually SD or HQ — no tool can recover pixels YouTube does not have.

    Using a downloaded thumbnail fairly

    • A thumbnail is the creator’s artwork and copyright: re-uploading it as your own video’s thumbnail (or in ads/products) infringes, even though downloading it took two clicks.
    • Clearly legitimate uses: embedding/linking with credit for commentary, reviews, news reporting, presentations about the video, or studying what makes it clickable for your own designs.
    • Need just the look, not the asset? Recreate the composition with your own screenshots and text — most thumbnail styles (big face, 3-word text, high contrast) are techniques, not protectable assets.

    Frequently asked questions

    Which thumbnail sizes can I download?

    Up to five: Maximum 1280×720 (maxresdefault), SD 640×480, HQ 480×360, MQ 320×180 and Default 120×90. Not every video has the largest size — unavailable sizes are hidden automatically.

    Is it legal to re-use a YouTube thumbnail?

    Thumbnails are usually the creator’s copyrighted work. Downloading for reference or inspiration is common; re-publishing someone else’s thumbnail as your own can infringe copyright and mislead viewers. When in doubt, make your own.

    Does this work for Shorts and private videos?

    Shorts links work (the thumbnail may be a vertical crop). Private or deleted videos have no public thumbnail, so nothing will load for them.
