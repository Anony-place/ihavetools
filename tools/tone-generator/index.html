Tone Generator

Generate test tones from 1 Hz to 20 kHz in four waveforms. Play them through your speakers or download a studio-standard 16-bit WAV file. Great for hearing tests, tuning instruments and testing audio gear.

This tool runs entirely in your browser. Your data is never uploaded or stored.

How to use Tone Generator

  1. Choose a waveform and type a frequency (A4 = 440 Hz).
  2. Press Play to hear it, or Download WAV to save it.
  3. Adjust volume and duration as needed.
