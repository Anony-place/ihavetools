    Work Hours Calculator

    Calculate work time between clock-in and clock-out, subtract an unpaid break, handle overnight shifts and estimate gross hourly pay.

    Calculations and text processing happen in your browser. Optional share links can contain inputs; see the Privacy Policy.

    How to use Work Hours Calculator

    1. Choose clock-in and clock-out times and enter unpaid break minutes.
    2. Optionally enter an hourly rate, or use zero.
    3. Read paid hours in clock and decimal format and the estimated gross pay.

    Work Hours Calculator guide

    Work hours and decimal hours

    Subtract start from end, treating an earlier end as the next day; then subtract unpaid break minutes. Divide paid minutes by 60 for decimal hours. The hourly estimate is paid decimal hours multiplied by hourly rate.

    Daytime and overnight examples

    09:00 to 17:00 is 8 hours; with a 60-minute unpaid break, paid time is 7 hours. 22:00 to 06:00 with a 30-minute break gives 7 hours 30 minutes, or 7.50 decimal hours.

    Payroll is more than clock arithmetic

    • Enter only unpaid breaks; your employer’s rules control which breaks are paid.
    • The tool does not calculate overtime, different shift rates or legal entitlements.
    • There is no date or time-zone input, so daylight-saving transitions are not modeled.
    • A share link contains the entered times and rate; avoid sharing private details unintentionally.

    Frequently asked questions

    Are overnight shifts supported?

    Yes. An end time earlier than the start is treated as the next day. Equal start and end times mean zero hours, not a 24-hour shift.

    Does the calculator include overtime or taxes?

    No. It multiplies paid hours by the entered hourly rate. Overtime, holiday rates, taxes, deductions and local payroll rules are not applied.

    Does it account for daylight saving?

    No. It uses wall-clock times without dates or time zones. Actual elapsed time across a daylight-saving change can differ.
