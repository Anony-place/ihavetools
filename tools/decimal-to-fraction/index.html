    Decimal to Fraction Converter

    Convert a finite decimal into an exact simplified fraction, or use long division to show a terminating or repeating decimal from a fraction.

    Calculations and text processing happen in your browser. Optional share links can contain inputs; see the Privacy Policy.

    How to use Decimal to Fraction Converter

    1. Enter a finite decimal and select Convert decimal to fraction.
    2. Alternatively enter a fraction and select Convert fraction to decimal.
    3. Read the simplified fraction or decimal; parentheses mark repeating digits.

    Decimal to Fraction Converter guide

    Convert a decimal to a fraction

    Count the digits after the decimal point and use the corresponding power of ten as the denominator. For example, 0.125 is 125/1000, which simplifies to 1/8. The calculator uses exact integers rather than binary floating-point approximations.

    Convert a fraction to a decimal

    Long division of 3/8 gives 0.375. For 1/6 the result is 0.1(6): the digits in parentheses repeat indefinitely. A minus sign applies to the whole result.

    Limits and notation

    • Decimal input must be finite, with at most 100 digits. Scientific notation and repeating-decimal input are not supported.
    • Fraction input accepts a signed integer numerator and a positive integer denominator, with at most 100 digits each. Mixed numbers are not accepted here.
    • Long division stops after 200 fractional digits. An ellipsis means the expansion was truncated, not rounded; it may terminate or repeat beyond that point.
    • This tool runs in the browser. Copying a result places it on your clipboard.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is 0.125 as a fraction?

    0.125 is 125/1000, which simplifies exactly to 1/8.

    What do parentheses in the decimal mean?

    They mark digits that repeat forever. For example, 1/3 is 0.(3), and 1/6 is 0.1(6).

    Can I enter 0.(3) as a decimal?

    No. The decimal input accepts finite decimal notation only. The fraction-to-decimal direction can display detected repeating cycles.

    Does an ellipsis mean the decimal repeats?

    Not necessarily. It means the expansion was truncated at 200 fractional digits; termination or repetition can occur later.

    What is Decimal to Fraction Converter used for?

    Convert a finite decimal into an exact simplified fraction, or use long division to show a terminating or repeating decimal from a fraction.
