Sitemap Generator

Turn a list of URLs into a valid XML sitemap with optional lastmod, changefreq and priority. Includes hreflang and entry validation.

This tool runs entirely in your browser. Your data is never uploaded or stored.

How to use Sitemap Generator

  1. Paste your URLs, one per line.
  2. Set optional lastmod, changefreq and priority defaults.
  3. Download the generated sitemap.xml for your site root.
