    BMI Calculator

    Calculate Body Mass Index from height and weight in metric or imperial units, with the WHO weight category and the healthy BMI range for your height.

    This tool runs entirely in your browser. Your data is never uploaded or stored.

    How to use BMI Calculator

    1. Choose metric (cm/kg) or imperial (ft+in/lb).
    2. Enter your height and weight.
    3. Read your BMI, category and healthy weight range.

    BMI Calculator guide

    The BMI formula — and a worked example

    BMI = weight in kilograms ÷ (height in metres)². Example: at 170 cm and 70 kg, BMI = 70 ÷ 1.70² = 24.2 — just inside the healthy range. For that height, the healthy weight band works out to roughly 53.5–72 kg, which is the range this calculator shows you.

    BMI categories for adults (WHO)

    BMICategory
    Below 18.5Underweight
    18.5 – 24.9Healthy weight
    25.0 – 29.9Overweight
    30.0 and aboveObesity

    What BMI doesn’t tell you

    • Muscle vs fat: athletes routinely score “overweight” with very low body fat, because muscle is dense.
    • Where fat sits: abdominal (visceral) fat carries more health risk than the same weight spread elsewhere — waist size adds context BMI lacks.
    • Build and ancestry: several Asian health guidelines suggest extra caution from a BMI of 23 upward, because metabolic risk rises earlier at the same BMI.
    • Age: older adults naturally lose muscle, so a “healthy” BMI can hide low muscle mass. Use BMI as one signal among several — and see a doctor for medical conclusions.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is a healthy BMI?

    For most adults, 18.5–24.9 is the healthy range. Below 18.5 is underweight, 25–29.9 is overweight and 30 or above is classed as obesity. The healthy weight range shown by this tool is simply those BMI bounds converted back to kilograms for your height.

    Is BMI accurate for athletes and bodybuilders?

    Not really. BMI can’t tell muscle from fat, so muscular people often land in “overweight” despite low body fat — and people with little muscle can score “healthy” while carrying excess fat. Treat BMI as a rough screening number, not a diagnosis.

    Does BMI work for children?

    Children and teenagers use BMI-for-age percentile charts instead, because healthy body composition changes as they grow. The adult cut-offs in this calculator (18.5 / 25 / 30) should not be applied to under-18s.
