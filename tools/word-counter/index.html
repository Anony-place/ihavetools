    Word Counter guide

    How reading time is calculated

    The average adult reads silently at roughly 200 words per minute, so this tool divides your word count by 200. Reading aloud is slower — about 130–150 words per minute — which is the number to use when timing a speech, presentation or video script. A 5-minute talk is therefore roughly 650–750 words, not 1,000.

    Novel chapter2,000–4,000 words

    Word count or character count?

    Essays, articles and assignments are almost always limited by words. Social posts, SMS, form fields and SEO titles are limited by characters — sometimes counting spaces, sometimes not. This tool shows both (characters with and without spaces); for platform-by-platform limits, use the Character Counter instead.

    Frequently asked questions

    How is reading time calculated?

    Words are divided by 200 — the average adult silent reading speed — and rounded up to the nearest 5 seconds.

    What counts as a sentence?

    Text separated by sentence-ending punctuation (. ! ? …). Abbreviations without punctuation are not split.

    What is Word Counter used for?

    Count words, characters (with and without spaces), sentences, paragraphs, lines and reading time as you type. Nothing is uploaded.
