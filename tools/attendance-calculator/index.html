    Attendance Calculator

    Calculate current attendance percentage and exactly how many upcoming classes you must attend to reach a target — or how many you can miss while staying safely above it.

    Calculations and text processing happen in your browser. Optional share links can contain inputs; see the Privacy Policy.

    How to use Attendance Calculator

    1. Enter classes held so far and classes you attended.
    2. Set your institution’s target — commonly 75%, but use your actual rule.
    3. Read your current percentage and either the recovery streak needed or your safe miss buffer.

    Attendance Calculator guide

    Every future class changes both sides of the fraction

    Attendance is attended ÷ held × 100. A class you attend adds 1 to both numbers; a class you miss adds 1 only to “held”. To recover to a target T, the calculator solves (attended + x) ÷ (held + x) ≥ T for whole classes. That is why at 28/40, just “earning back the missing 5%” takes 8 straight classes, ending at 36/48 = 75%.

    Attendance situations at a 75% target

    Attended / heldNowWhat the maths says
    28 / 4070%Attend 8 in a row → 36/48 = 75%
    32 / 4080%Can miss 2 → 32/42 = 76.19%; a third drops below
    39 / 4097.5%Safe for 75%, but exactly 100% can never be recovered

    Keep a buffer, not a knife-edge

    • At a 75% target, one miss needs three attended classes to restore the same ratio — recovery is slower than most students expect.
    • Portal totals can lag, labs may carry separate minimums, and some institutions round or impose subject-wise rules. Treat “can miss” as a ceiling, not a plan.
    • The recovery streak assumes enough classes remain in the term. If the required streak is longer than the timetable left, the target is mathematically out of reach and you should speak to the department early.

    Frequently asked questions

    I have 28 of 40 classes. How do I reach 75%?

    You are at 70%. Attend the next 8 classes in a row: 36 attended out of 48 held is exactly 75%. Missing one during the recovery makes the required streak longer because the denominator grows too.

    Why can I miss only 2 classes at 32 out of 40?

    At 32/40 you are at 80%. Missing 2 makes it 32/42 = 76.19%, still over 75%; missing a third makes it 32/43 = 74.42%, below target. The calculator counts whole future classes, not a rounded percentage.

    Does medical leave count as attended?

    That depends entirely on your school or university’s condonation policy. Enter the official attended/held figures from your portal after any approved leave adjustment; this tool cannot grant or predict condonation.
