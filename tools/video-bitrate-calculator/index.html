    Video Bitrate Calculator

    Estimate video file size from average video and audio bitrate, or calculate a video bitrate budget for a target size and duration.

    Calculations and text processing happen in your browser. Optional share links can contain inputs; see the Privacy Policy.

    How to use Video Bitrate Calculator

    1. Choose file-size estimate or target-size bitrate mode.
    2. Enter duration in seconds, audio bitrate and video bitrate or target size.
    3. Calculate and allow extra space for container overhead and variable bitrate.

    Video Bitrate Calculator guide

    Estimate from average bitrate

    Add video Mbps and audio kbps divided by 1000. Multiply by seconds and divide by 8 to estimate decimal MB. For 600 seconds at 10 Mbps video and 128 kbps audio the estimate is 759.6 MB.

    Budget for a target size

    Divide target MB by duration, multiply by 8, and subtract audio Mbps. A 750 MB target over 600 seconds with 128 kbps audio leaves 9.872 Mbps for video before overhead.

    What this estimate excludes

    • This is not an encoder or compressor and does not inspect a video. Actual sizes vary with variable bitrate, container overhead, extra tracks, metadata and encoder settings. Leave headroom under hard upload limits.
    • MB means 1,000,000 bytes; GiB means 1,073,741,824 bytes. Enter average rather than peak bitrates, total audio bitrate across tracks, and duration in seconds.

    Frequently asked questions

    Does this calculate quality?

    No. Visual quality depends on the codec, resolution, frame rate, content and encoder settings as well as bitrate.

    Does this include container overhead?

    No. The result is an ideal estimate from bitrates and duration; leave extra room for container overhead and bitrate variation.

    Why is GiB different from MB?

    Decimal MB uses one million bytes while binary GiB uses 1,073,741,824 bytes.

    What if audio exceeds the target size?

    The bitrate budget is impossible; lower audio bitrate, shorten the duration or increase the target.
