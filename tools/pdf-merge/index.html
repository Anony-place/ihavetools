    PDF Merge

    Merge multiple PDF files into one document, in your chosen order. Files are processed locally with pdf-lib; nothing is uploaded to any server.

    Your file is processed locally in your browser and is not uploaded.

    How to use PDF Merge

    1. Drop or choose two or more PDF files.
    2. Reorder them by dragging or with the arrow buttons if needed.
    3. Click Merge PDFs, then download the combined file.

    Why use the iHaveTools PDF Merge?

    • Private by design — Your file is processed locally in your browser and is not uploaded.
    • Free and unlimited — no sign-up, no watermarks, no daily limits and nothing to install.
    • Works everywhere — any modern browser on a phone, tablet or computer, ready the moment the page opens.

    People also search for this tool as “merge pdf”, “combine pdf”, “join pdf” — it is the same free online tool. PDF Merge is part of our PDF & Documents tools collection.

    Frequently asked questions

    Are my PDFs uploaded anywhere?

    No. Merging runs entirely in your browser using the pdf-lib library, which is loaded once from this site. Your files stay on your device.

    Is there a file size or page limit?

    There is no artificial limit — the constraint is your device’s memory, since pages are copied into a new document in memory.

    What is PDF Merge used for?

    Merge multiple PDF files into one document, in your chosen order. Files are processed locally with pdf-lib; nothing is uploaded to any server.

    Is “merge pdf” the same as PDF Merge?

    Yes — “merge pdf” is another name people use for this tool. This page is the free online PDF Merge: open it and use it right away, with nothing to download or sign up for.

    Do I need to install anything to use PDF Merge?

    No. PDF Merge runs in any modern web browser — on a phone, tablet or computer — as soon as the page opens.
