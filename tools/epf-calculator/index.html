    EPF Calculator

    Calculate your Employees’ Provident Fund balance at retirement from basic salary plus DA, current balance, salary growth and expected interest — with the employer’s EPS diversion shown separately.

    Calculations and text processing happen in your browser. Optional share links can contain inputs; see the Privacy Policy.

    How to use EPF Calculator

    1. Enter monthly basic salary plus DA — not gross CTC — and your current EPF balance.
    2. Set current age, retirement age, expected salary growth and EPF interest.
    3. Read the projected balance split into your share, the employer’s EPF share, EPS diversion and interest.

    EPF Calculator guide

    Where each month’s PF money goes

    That split is why a ₹50,000 basic + DA adds ₹10,750 a month to EPF, not ₹12,000: 12% comes from you, 12% from the employer, and ₹1,250 of the employer share goes to EPS instead. EPS may create a separate pension entitlement; it is not part of the EPF balance this calculator projects.

    Monthly basic + DAYou contribute to EPFEmployer to EPFEmployer diverted to EPS

    ₹50,000₹6,000₹4,750₹1,250 (cap reached)

    A worked 20-year example

    Start with no balance, monthly basic + DA of ₹30,000, 5% yearly salary growth and an assumed 8.25% EPF interest rate. Over 20 years, this calculator projects your contributions at ₹14,28,449, the employer’s EPF share at ₹11,28,449 and interest at ₹28,58,404 — a balance of ₹54,15,302. Another ₹3,00,000 of employer money is diverted to EPS during those 20 years and is deliberately excluded from the EPF total.

    How interest is approximated here

    • EPF interest is based on monthly running balances and credited annually, rather than compounded into the account every month.
    • This tool follows that pattern approximately: interest accrues through the year on the evolving balance and is added at year end.
    • Salary growth is applied once each year, so monthly contributions rise in steps rather than continuously.
    • Withdrawals, loans/advances, unemployment breaks and employer-specific wage caps are not modelled — adjust the salary or current balance to explore “what if” cases.

    Frequently asked questions

    Why is the employer’s EPF contribution less than 12%?

    The employer also contributes 12% in total, but part of it is diverted to the Employees’ Pension Scheme (EPS). Under the standard wage-ceiling assumption used here, EPS is 8.33% of basic + DA capped at ₹1,250 a month; the remaining employer amount goes into EPF.

    Why may my EPF passbook differ from this estimate?

    Your employer may cap PF wages, your actual basic + DA may differ, or there may be service breaks, advances, withdrawals, transfers or joining-date EPS differences. Interest is declared year by year and credited according to EPFO timelines, while this calculator uses one expected rate.

    What salary should I enter if my company caps PF at ₹15,000?

    Enter ₹15,000 as monthly basic + DA for PF purposes, even if your payslip basic is higher. The calculator follows the contribution base you enter; it cannot infer your employer’s payroll policy.
