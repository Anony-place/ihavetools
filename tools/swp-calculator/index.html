    SWP Calculator

    Estimate systematic withdrawal plan cash flow, investment growth, remaining corpus and the month of depletion with a configurable return.

    Calculations and text processing happen in your browser. Optional share links can contain inputs; see the Privacy Policy.

    How to use SWP Calculator

    1. Enter your starting corpus and desired monthly withdrawal.
    2. Set a constant annual return assumption and the projection period in months.
    3. Review total withdrawn, estimated growth, remaining balance and any depletion month.

    SWP Calculator guide

    How this SWP projection works

    Each month the model applies the fixed annual return divided by 12 to the remaining balance, then withdraws the requested amount or whatever remains. It stops at depletion. This is a simplified nominal monthly model, not a fund forecast.

    Zero-return example

    A 100,000 corpus with 10,000 withdrawn monthly and no growth lasts exactly 10 months. Total withdrawn is 100,000 and the remaining balance is zero. If the withdrawal were 30,000, the fourth and final withdrawal would be 10,000.

    Why actual results can differ

    • Market returns move unevenly; early losses can reduce how long a withdrawal plan lasts.
    • Tax on redemptions, expense ratios and exit loads are not included.
    • A fixed withdrawal loses purchasing power with inflation.
    • Test conservative assumptions and keep a cash buffer instead of treating a projection as a promise.

    Frequently asked questions

    Does an SWP guarantee monthly income?

    No. An SWP withdraws from the investment, including principal when necessary. Returns fluctuate and the corpus can run out.

    How is a partial final withdrawal handled?

    If the balance after that month’s assumed growth is less than the requested withdrawal, only the available balance is withdrawn and the corpus is marked depleted.

    Are taxes and exit loads included?

    No. Tax, exit loads, fees, inflation and variable returns are excluded. Consult relevant fund documents and tax guidance before investing.
