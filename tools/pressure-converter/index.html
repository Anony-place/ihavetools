    Pressure Converter guide

    Pressure units and conversion

    Pressure is force per area. The pascal is one newton per square metre; one bar is 100,000 pascals. To convert, multiply by the source unit’s pascal factor and divide by the target factor.

    Worked example

    One bar equals 100,000 Pa. Dividing by approximately 6,894.757293 Pa per psi gives about 14.5038 psi.

    Keep the reference consistent

    • A gauge reading is relative to ambient pressure; an absolute reading is relative to a vacuum. Unit conversion does not change that reference.
    • A standard atmosphere is a convention, not a live barometer reading.
    • Results use floating-point arithmetic and are rounded for display; check safety-critical pressure values independently.

    Frequently asked questions

    How many psi are in one bar?

    One bar is approximately 14.50377377 psi.

    Is gauge pressure the same as absolute pressure?

    No. This tool converts the numerical value only. It does not add or subtract atmospheric pressure; keep the same reference when converting.

    What is a standard atmosphere?

    A standard atmosphere is exactly 101,325 pascals. Local atmospheric pressure can differ.
