    Business Day Calculator

    Count business days between dates or add and subtract working days with selectable weekends and manually entered holidays.

    Calculations and text processing happen in your browser. Optional share links can contain inputs; see the Privacy Policy.

    How to use Business Day Calculator

    1. Choose the start date, weekend pattern and any holiday dates.
    2. For a range, choose the end date and count both endpoints.
    3. For a deadline, enter a positive or negative offset and add or subtract working days.

    Business Day Calculator guide

    Counting and deadlines use different conventions

    The range count includes both start and end, removing selected weekend days and manually entered holidays. For an offset, start with the next date in the chosen direction; do not count the start date. Negative offsets move backwards, and zero keeps the original date.

    Worked example with a holiday

    From Friday 6 November to Friday 13 November 2026 inclusive, the Saturday–Sunday weekend leaves six weekdays. If 11 November is entered as a holiday, five working days remain. Adding five working days to 6 November with that holiday lands on Monday 16 November.

    Confirm your calendar

    • No public holiday database is loaded: enter the actual days relevant to your jurisdiction and workplace.
    • Some deadlines count the starting day or roll an end date differently; follow the contract or official rule, not a generic convention.
    • Weekend holidays are not double-counted. Duplicate holiday entries are ignored.
    • The calculation uses calendar dates rather than elapsed hours, so daylight-saving clock changes do not affect the result.

    Frequently asked questions

    Are public holidays included automatically?

    No. Enter holiday dates yourself in YYYY-MM-DD format. Holiday rules vary by country, state and employer, so verify your list against an authoritative calendar.

    Are start and end dates included?

    Counting includes both dates if they are working days. Adding or subtracting a nonzero number excludes the start date; zero returns the start date even if it is non-working.

    Can I change the weekend?

    Yes. Choose Saturday–Sunday, Friday–Saturday, Sunday only or no weekend. Holidays on a weekend are excluded once, not twice.

    What are the supported date limits?

    Dates must be between 1900 and 2100. A counting range is limited to 36,600 calendar days, offsets to 10,000 working days and holiday input to 500 dates.
