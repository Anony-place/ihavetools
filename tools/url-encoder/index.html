    URL Encoder & Decoder

    Encode or decode URLs and URL components. Component mode escapes every reserved character; full-URL mode keeps separators like / and ? intact.

    Everything is generated or computed locally in your browser with its cryptographic APIs. Nothing is sent anywhere.

    How to use URL Encoder & Decoder

    1. Paste text or an encoded URL.
    2. Choose Component (values) or Full URL, and Encode or Decode.
    3. Copy the result. Invalid sequences are reported clearly.
