    1. Enter monthly basic salary plus DA, the HRA shown in your salary structure and rent actually paid.

    3. Read exempt HRA, taxable HRA and which of the three limits decided the result.

    HRA Calculator guide

    The lowest of three numbers is exempt

    Section 10(13A) compares: (1) actual HRA received, (2) rent paid minus 10% of salary, and (3) 50% of salary for the four specified metro cities or 40% elsewhere. Here “salary” means basic pay plus DA that forms part of retirement benefits (and fixed-percentage turnover commission where applicable), not the whole CTC.

    Worked example: rent is the deciding limit

    Annual figureAmount
    Basic + DA (₹50,000 × 12)₹6,00,000
    Actual HRA (₹20,000 × 12)₹2,40,000
    Rent − 10% of basic + DA (₹2,40,000 − ₹60,000)₹1,80,000 — lowest
    Metro salary cap (50% of ₹6,00,000)₹3,00,000
    Exempt / taxable HRA₹1,80,000 / ₹60,000

    Before you submit proofs

    • You must actually pay rent for accommodation you occupy; merely having HRA in the salary structure is not enough.
    • Keep the rent agreement, payment trail and rent receipts. Employers commonly ask for the landlord’s PAN when annual rent exceeds ₹1 lakh.
    • If your rent is less than 10% of basic + DA, the second limit becomes zero and no HRA exemption is available.
    • Mid-year salary revisions, city changes and rent changes should be calculated period by period by payroll — this tool assumes the monthly amounts stay constant for a full year.

    Frequently asked questions

    Which cities get the 50% HRA limit?

    For HRA exemption, the 50% of basic + DA limit applies to rented homes in Mumbai, Kolkata, Delhi and Chennai. Other large cities — including Bengaluru, Hyderabad and Pune — use the 40% limit for this calculation.

    Can I claim HRA under the new tax regime?

    HRA exemption is an old-regime benefit. If you file under the default new regime, this calculation can still help compare regimes, but the exempt amount is not separately subtracted in the same way.

    Can I claim HRA if I live with my parents?

    Potentially, if you genuinely pay them rent for a home they own and can document it. The rent becomes rental income for the parent receiving it. You cannot create a paper-only rent claim while owning and occupying the same house yourself.
