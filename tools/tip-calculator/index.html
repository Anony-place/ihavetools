Tip Calculator

Calculate a tip from a bill with quick presets, round options, and an even per-person split for groups.

This tool runs entirely in your browser. Your data is never uploaded or stored.

How to use Tip Calculator

  1. Enter the bill amount and pick or type a tip percentage.
  2. Set the number of people and optional rounding.
  3. Read tip, total and the amount each person pays.
