    Date Add / Subtract

    Add or subtract whole calendar days, weeks, months or years from a date with month-end clamping.

    Calculations and text processing happen in your browser. Optional share links can contain inputs; see the Privacy Policy.

    How to use Date Add / Subtract

    1. Pick a start date.
    2. Enter a positive whole amount, unit and direction.
    3. Read the resulting date and weekday.

    Date Add / Subtract guide

    Calendar rather than business days

    Adding days or weeks moves through every calendar date, including weekends and holidays. For working days with manual holidays, use the Business Day Calculator.

    Month-end example

    31 January 2024 plus one month is 29 February 2024, because February has 29 days that year. The same calculation in 2023 ends on 28 February.

    Check the convention

    • Enter a positive whole number and select the direction.
    • Month and year operations clamp once to the target month; adding and then subtracting may not recover the original date.
    • Input and result dates must be between 1900 and 2100. Unix seconds use the browser’s local midnight.

    Frequently asked questions

    How are month-end dates handled?

    When the target month lacks the original day, the result is clamped to its final day. January 31 plus one month is February 28 or 29.

    Can I use fractional or negative amounts?

    No. Enter a positive whole number and choose Add or Subtract. The supported amount is 1 to 100,000 and dates must remain in 1900–2100.

    Does adding one year preserve February 29?

    If the target year is not a leap year, February 29 is clamped to February 28.

    What does Unix seconds mean here?

    It represents the browser’s local start of that date, not UTC midnight. Time-zone rules can shift or skip local midnight; if an entire local date is skipped, the tool reports an error.
