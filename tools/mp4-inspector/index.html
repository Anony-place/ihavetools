MP4 Inspector

Inspect MP4, M4V and MOV files without uploading them: duration, resolution, video and audio codecs, average bitrate, brand and the full top-level box structure, parsed byte-by-byte in your browser.

This tool runs entirely in your browser. Your data is never uploaded or stored.

How to use MP4 Inspector

  1. Drop in an MP4, M4V or MOV file.
  2. Read duration, resolution, codecs and bitrate instantly.
  3. Browse the top-level MP4 boxes with their sizes.

Yes — it is completely free, with no sign-up, no watermarks and no usage limits.
