About iHaveTools

84 free tools. No sign-up, no uploads, no limits — everything runs in your browser.

What is iHaveTools?

iHaveTools is a collection of 84 free online tools for everyday work: calculators, converters, text and image utilities, PDF tools and developer helpers. Every tool works instantly in your web browser — there is no account to create, nothing to install and nothing to pay.

How it works — and why your data stays private

Unlike most tool sites, iHaveTools has no server that processes your work. When you compress an image, merge a PDF or format JSON here, the computation happens on your own device using built-in browser technology (Canvas, Web Audio, Web Crypto and pdf-lib). Your files and text are never uploaded, so there is nothing for us to store, lose or leak. You can verify this yourself: once a tool page has loaded, it keeps working with your device offline.

Our principles

  • Private by design — your data never leaves your device. See the Privacy Policy.
  • Free and unlimited — no sign-up walls, watermarks or daily limits.
  • Fast — static pages, no frameworks, no trackers in your way.
  • Honest — every tool listed on this site genuinely works; there are no empty placeholder pages.

Contact & feedback

Found a bug, or want a tool we do not have yet? The project is open source — open an issue on GitHub. The full source code is available in the same repository, so anyone can audit exactly what the site does with data (nothing).
